Avoid Online Earning Scams

Never send money simply because someone promises a guaranteed job or guaranteed income.

  • Do not pay a recruitment fee for a job without independently verifying the employer.
  • Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords or card PINs.
  • Be suspicious of guaranteed daily-income promises.
  • Verify the company website and contact details.
  • Use official application websites whenever possible.
  • Do not install unknown APK files sent through WhatsApp or Telegram.
