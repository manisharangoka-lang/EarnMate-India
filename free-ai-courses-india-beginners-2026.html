7 Free AI Courses in India for Beginners in 2026

Start learning artificial intelligence with free introductory courses from government and established learning providers. This guide compares the topics, entry requirements and certificate details that are clear from official course information.

Last reviewed: 30 October 2026 · Estimated reading time: 7–9 minutes

AI is now used in writing, education, customer support, data analysis and many other areas. You can begin learning the basics without paying for a course or knowing how to code. Choose one course that fits your goal, practise what you learn, and verify the current terms on the provider’s site before enrolling.

Realistic expectations: Completing a course or earning a certificate does not guarantee a job or income. Course access, languages, assessments and certificate rules can change. Confirm the latest details with the official provider.

1. YUVA AI for ALL

Launched by MeitY under the IndiaAI Mission, YUVA AI for ALL is a self-paced introduction designed for students, professionals and other curious learners. The Government of India announcement describes it as a free 4.5-hour course with six modules covering AI basics, uses and responsible use.

BeginnerAbout 4.5 hoursNo course fee stated by government

Eligibility: The announcement describes it as open to everyone; no technical background is stated as necessary.

Certificate: The announcement says learners who complete the course receive an official Government of India certificate. Follow the selected platform’s completion steps.

2–4. Google’s beginner AI courses

2. Introduction to Generative AI

A short introduction to what generative AI is, how people use it, and how it differs from traditional machine learning. Google describes this as a free beginner course; its India AI skills page points learners to a short quiz for Google Cloud credentials.

Eligibility: Recommended for everyone; no coding background is indicated. Cost: Free course. The listing does not state a separate fee for this introductory course credential.

3. Introduction to Large Language Models

Learn what large language models are and explore common uses such as generating and summarising text. Google lists a free course for beginners and a Google Cloud badge obtainable after a short quiz.

Eligibility: Everyone, beginners to AI. Cost: Google lists the learning resource as free; check the activity page for any account or assessment requirements.

4. Introduction to Responsible AI

Explore why responsible AI matters and how principles such as safety and fairness guide the development and use of AI systems. Google lists this as a free beginner course with a quiz for a Google Cloud badge.

Eligibility: Everyone, beginners to AI. Cost: Free learning resource; credential requirements are set on the provider’s current page.

5. Microsoft Learn: AI concepts for developers and technology professionals

This seven-module beginner learning path introduces generative AI and agents, computer vision, speech, natural language processing, information extraction and retrieval-augmented generation.

Beginner level7 modulesFree self-paced learning

Prerequisites: Microsoft recommends a basic understanding of computing concepts and mathematics, so absolute beginners may prefer to start with YUVA AI for ALL or Google’s introductory course.

Certificate: Microsoft Learn may show achievements for learning activity. These are distinct from a paid, proctored Microsoft certification exam; confirm the available achievement on the learning page.

6. IBM SkillsBuild: AI Literacy

IBM describes AI Literacy as a short, jargon-free introduction in two modules. It covers how AI systems learn, how people and AI work together, and examples across industries. The activity is aimed at students, job seekers, educators and other curious learners.

Eligibility: General learners; review the course page for account requirements. Cost: IBM SkillsBuild describes its learning as free. Credential: IBM labels AI Literacy as earning a digital credential; complete the provider’s listed requirements.

7. Elements of AI: Introduction to AI

Elements of AI is a free online course that introduces core AI ideas through explanations and exercises. It suits learners who want to understand what AI is, rather than only learning how to operate AI tools.

Eligibility: The introductory course is designed for general learners. Cost: The course and its digital certificate are free. Learners must meet the course’s exercise completion and passing requirements.

Open Elements of AI ↗

Which course should you start with?

  • Want an India-focused overview? Start with YUVA AI for ALL.
  • Want a short introduction? Try Google’s Introduction to Generative AI.
  • Use AI chatbots and want to understand them? Study the Google LLM course.
  • Want broader technical topics? Try Microsoft Learn after a basic introduction.
  • Want a deeper conceptual course? Explore Elements of AI.

You do not need to finish all seven. Complete one course, write down what you learned, and make a small project such as a fact-checked study guide or a sample FAQ. Keep your project in a portfolio to demonstrate how you applied the ideas.

Can a free AI course help you get a job?

A course can build foundational knowledge, but it does not by itself qualify you for every AI role. Job requirements vary. Depending on the role, employers may also expect communication, spreadsheets, data analysis, programming, writing, design or customer-service skills. Look at genuine job descriptions, then build relevant examples of your work. Do not pay anyone who promises a guaranteed job or income because you completed a course.

Frequently asked questions

Can I learn AI for free in India?

Yes. The providers above list free introductory learning options. Confirm current access conditions and any optional credential costs directly with each provider.

Do I need coding experience?

No coding is needed to begin with the introductory AI literacy courses. Programming becomes useful for building AI applications and pursuing some technical roles.

Are all certificates free?

No single rule applies to every platform. The Government of India announcement says YUVA AI for ALL completers receive an official certificate. Google describes free courses with credentials available through quizzes. Elements of AI says its Introduction to AI certificate is free when course requirements are met. Microsoft and IBM credential requirements depend on the specific learning activity, so check its current course page before enrolling.

Will a certificate guarantee a job?

No. It can document learning, but employment depends on the role, your skills, experience, projects and other requirements.

Final recommendation

If you are new to AI, begin with YUVA AI for ALL or Google’s Introduction to Generative AI. Then explore responsible AI and build a small project. Use official provider pages to confirm current enrolment, language, assessment and credential terms.
